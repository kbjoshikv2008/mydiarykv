1. Find the number of proper subsets of the set $\{p, q, r, s\}$.

3. Assertion & Reason based:

Assertion (A): $\cos x$ is positive in first and fourth quadrants.

Reason (R): $\tan x$ increases from $0$ to $\infty$ in first quadrant and increases from $0$ to $\infty$ in third quadrant.

a. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A. b. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A. c. A is true but R is false. d. A is false but R is true.
4. Find the value of $(i^{19})^{-4} + \frac{1}{i^{-13}}$.
5. Write the range of the function $f(x) = \frac{|x|}{x}$, $x \neq 0$.
SECTION – B (2 Marks each) [6 × 2 = 12]

8. A pendulum of length 60 cm swings and its tip describes an arc of 22 cm. Find the angle in radians.

SECTION – C (3 Marks each) [5 × 3 = 15]

Case Study (Trigonometry) [4]

Trigonometry – from Greek ‘Trigon’ (triangle) and ‘metron’ (measure) – studies relationships between angles and sides. Degree and radian units are used. $\pi$ radian = $180^\circ$; $1^\circ = 60'$ (minutes), $1' = 60''$ (seconds); arc length $l = r\theta$.

Based on the above, answer the following questions:

(i) Convert $\frac{13}{36}$ radians into degrees, minutes and seconds. [1]
(ii) Convert $\frac{5\pi}{12}$ into degree measure. [1]
(iii) Find the length of the arc traced by the minute hand of a clock in 10 minutes, if the radius is 10.5 cm. [2]
OR If arcs of same length in two circles subtend angles $60^\circ$ and $75^\circ$ at the centre, find the ratio of their radii.
