Formula of the Day

$a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$

Meaning: Difference of Two Squares Identity

Variables: $a, b$: real numbers or algebraic expressions
Example: $99^2 - 1^2 = (99 - 1)(99 + 1) = 98 imes 100 = 9800$
Shortcut Trick: Convert large squares into sum and difference factors.
Common Mistake: Writing $(a - b)^2$ instead of $(a - b)(a + b)$.
Did You Know?
