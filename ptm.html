Class 11 — Session Ending Exam (SEE) Required Marks Analyzer

Official KVS Assessment Matrix: Minimum marks required in Session Ending Exam (SEE) and PT-2 for each student of Class 11 to secure 27/80 in Theory (minimum 27 passing in SEE paper), 7/20 in Internal Assessment, and 33/100 aggregate to pass.

KVS Class XI Mathematics Examination Passing & Weightage Scheme
Passing thresholds: Theory ≥ 27/80 (Paper minimum 27), IA ≥ 7/20, Combined ≥ 33/100

Theory Component (80 Marks)

• PT-1: 40 marks → weightage 10 (PT1/40 × 10)
• Half-Yearly: 80 marks → weightage 30 (HY/80 × 30)
• PT-2: 40 marks → weightage 10 (PT2/40 × 10) (Not conducted yet • Blank)
• SEE: 80 marks → weightage 50 (SEE/80 × 50) (Session Ending Exam • Blank)
Total Weightage: 100 → Reduced to 80 marks (× 0.8).
Passing Score: 27 out of 80 (33%)

Internal Assessment (20 Marks)

• PT Component: PT-1 & PT-2 combined & reduced to 10 marks ((PT1+PT2)/80 × 10)
• Lab Manual: Records of activities = 5 marks (Not conducted yet • Blank)
• Activity Exam & Viva: Activities & viva = 5 marks (Not conducted yet • Blank)
Total Internal Assessment: 20 marks.
Passing Score: 7 out of 20 (33%)

Passing & Eligibility Target

• Mandatory Paper Threshold: Minimum 27 marks out of 80 is mandatory in the SEE question paper to qualify, even if mathematically requiring less.
• Separate Passing: Student must pass separately in Theory (≥ 27/80) and Internal Assessment (≥ 7/20).
• Combined Score: Overall score must be ≥ 33 out of 100.
