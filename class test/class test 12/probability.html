[1 Mark]

1. If \( P(A) = 0.6, P(B) = 0.3, P(A \cap B) = 0.2 \), find \( P(A|B) \).

[1 Mark]

2. State Bayes Theorem formula.

[2 Marks]

3. Two cards are drawn without replacement from 52 cards. Find probability that both are aces.

[2 Marks]

4. If A and B are independent events with \( P(A) = 0.3, P(B) = 0.4 \), find \( P(A \cup B) \).

[2 Marks]

5. A coin is tossed 3 times. Find conditional probability of getting head on third toss given heads on first two tosses.

[4 Marks]

6. Bag I contains 3 red and 4 black balls, while Bag II contains 5 red and 6 black balls. One ball is drawn at random from one of the bags and it is found to be red. Find probability that it was drawn from Bag II.

[4 Marks]

7. A doctor is to visit a patient. From past experience, probabilities that he will come by train, bus, scooter or car are \( \frac{3}{10}, \frac{1}{5}, \frac{1}{10}, \frac{2}{5} \) respectively. Probabilities that he will be late are \( \frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{3}, \frac{1}{12}, \frac{1}{2} \) if he comes by train, bus, scooter and car. When he arrives, he is late. Find probability that he came by train.

[4 Marks]

8. Given three coins. One is a two-headed coin, another is a biased coin that comes up heads 75% of the time and third is an unbiased coin. One coin is chosen at random and tossed, it shows heads. What is the probability that it was the two-headed coin?
