Extension / Real life applications
Degree of polynomial, zeros of polynomial, Remainder Theorem, Factor Theorem, factorisation of cubic polynomials, algebraic identities. Students evaluate zeros, apply Remainder and Factor Theorems, and factorise quadratic & cubic polynomials. Synthetic division / long division comparison, factor theorem root testing, identity expansion drills. Algebraic Generalization, Root Analysis, Polynomial Factorization. Trajectory calculation in sports/ballistics, profit optimization curves, signal processing.

दक्षता आधारित पाठ योजना / Competency Based Lesson Plan

उद्देश्य / Objectives शिक्षण पद्धति / Teaching Methodology कौशल एवं दृष्टिकोण / Skills and Attitude दक्षताएँ / Competencies
1. Find zeros of linear and quadratic polynomials.
2. Apply Factor Theorem to test if (x-a) is a factor.
3. Factorise cubic polynomials using trial root + division.		 Zero Finding Workshop, Factor Theorem Substitution Drills, Cubic Polynomial Reduction. Structural algebraic thinking, systematic root search, polynomial manipulation. Polynomial Algebra, Remainder/Factor Theorem Mastery, Cubic Factorisation.
