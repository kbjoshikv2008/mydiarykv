दक्षता आधारित पाठ योजना / Competency Based Lesson Plan

उद्देश्य / Objectives शिक्षण पद्धति / Teaching Methodology कौशल एवं दृष्टिकोण / Skills and Attitude दक्षताएँ / Competencies

By the end of this chapter, students will be able to:

  • Apply derivatives to solve rate of change problems
  • Determine increasing/decreasing nature of functions
  • Find equations of tangents and normals to curves
  • Use derivatives for approximation calculations
  • Identify local maxima/minima using derivative tests
  • Solve practical optimization problems
  • Interpret results in real-world contexts

Periods 1-2: Rate of Change & Increasing/Decreasing Functions

  • Starter: Real-life rate examples (filling pool, spreading ripple)
  • Demonstration: Graphical interpretation of increasing/decreasing functions
  • Activity: Calculating rates of change from equations

Periods 3-4: Tangents, Normals & Approximations

  • Visualization: GeoGebra tangent/normal exploration
  • Group Work: Deriving tangent/normal equations
  • Application: Using derivatives for approximation calculations

Periods 5-8: Maxima/Minima & Optimization

  • Concept: First and second derivative tests
  • Case Studies: Business optimization problems
  • Project: Designing optimal containers (max volume)

Periods 9-12: Application & Review

  • Practical: Solving physics motion problems
  • Review: Concept mapping of derivative applications
  • Assessment: Chapter test and project evaluation
  • Analytical skills for problem decomposition
  • Critical thinking in optimization scenarios
  • Precision in mathematical calculations
  • Persistence in solving complex problems
  • Collaboration in group activities
  • Real-world application mindset
  • Systematic approach to multi-step problems

Competencies:

  • Mathematical reasoning and application
  • Critical thinking and problem-solving
  • Real-world contextualization
  • Collaborative learning
  • Digital literacy through technology use

Activities:

  • Optimization Challenge: Design most cost-efficient container
  • Business Simulation: Maximize profit with cost/revenue functions
  • Motion Analysis: Calculate velocity/acceleration from position functions
  • Error Estimation: Use derivatives for measurement approximations
